The Gap

The insurer's playbook and where we close it

Most policyholders lose value not because their claim is weak, but because the process is stacked against them. Here's what usually happens without an advocate in your corner.

What insurers count on

  • Policy language dense enough that key coverage goes unnoticed
  • An initial estimate scoped to the minimum, not the full damage
  • A homeowner with no baseline to know if the offer is fair
  • Time pressure that pushes you toward accepting quickly

What Veritas does instead

  • Reviews declarations, endorsements & exclusions clause by clause
  • Documents the full, code-compliant scope of repair
  • Gives you a clear, independent read on what's fair
  • Negotiates or invokes appraisal, until it's resolved right
Why Veritas

We read the policy insurers hope you won't.

Coverage is often there, buried in an endorsement or a depreciation clause most homeowners never see. Here's the kind of language we look for on every file.

Policy Excerpt, Section IIForm HO-3

Coverage includes ordinance or law coverage, subject to the limits shown on the declarations page, for increased costs of construction required by building code upon repair.

Losses settled on an actual cash value basis are eligible for recoverable depreciation once repairs are completed and documented, up to the policy limit.
